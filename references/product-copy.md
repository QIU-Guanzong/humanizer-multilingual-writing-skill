# Product copy and conversational follow-ups

Use this guide for product notices, data displays or assistant responses when the request supplies relevant product context. Do not infer a product's actual behavior from these examples. Keep copy short, but preserve the details a reader needs to understand the present state and next action.

## State and consequence

- Distinguish an action requested, submitted, awaiting confirmation, failed or completed. An interruption or stopped wait does not establish that the operation was cancelled; provider acknowledgement does not by itself establish that access was activated. Preserve separate actors and confirmation conditions.
- Distinguish missing, unavailable, unverified, outdated and zero data. Retain the relevant source and observation time; a page refresh time is not automatically the time the underlying data was observed. Do not convert a partial indicator into a complete sample, a probability or an endorsement.
- Keep where information is stored, how long it persists, what resets it, and which action actually saves, shares or deletes it. Do not imply a draft is saved to an account or public when only a local preview exists. Preserve the scope of a retry, cancellation or deletion and who is authorized to perform it.
- Preserve necessary limitations and disclosures even when removing repeated template text. Give a concrete next action when the source or brief establishes one; do not invent a retry, approval, refund or remedy. If the necessary state is unknown, say so without creating false confidence.

## Context and languages

- In a conversational follow-up, use the relevant established topic, time period and comparison from supplied context. Answer the current request; for an example request, provide a concrete, clearly hypothetical illustration of that topic when the supplied information supports one, rather than merely repeating the previous answer. Keep any necessary assumptions explicit; do not invent a unit or assert an assumed scenario as a real user experience. If the requested illustration cannot be made meaningful without an essential detail, ask for it. Do not reuse stale context after an explicit change or treat a user's correction as proof of an unrelated claim.
- Treat language, writing system, locale, register and a request for a spoken variety as separate choices. A quotation in another variety remains quoted text and is not automatically a request to change the response language.
- If the user supplies multiple language versions or asks to compare them, compare the substantive claims, lists, qualifications, conditions and action labels item by item. Flag omissions and unresolved differences before proposing corrections. Do not invent the missing terms: where the authoritative wording or product behavior is unclear, ask or mark the mismatch for verification. Comparing supplied versions does not authorize translation, publishing or a change to the product.

## Illustrative checks

- “Payment was acknowledged; access is awaiting verification.” must not become “Your access is active.”
- “Last saved value; the source has not updated, so it is not live.” must not become “Live value.”
- “The preview resets on refresh and is not saved to your account.” must retain both the reset condition and account-saving limitation.
- If one version lists “price, benefits and refunds” and another lists only “price and refunds,” report the missing benefits item rather than silently assuming what the payment terms are.
